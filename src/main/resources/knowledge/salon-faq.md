# Nail Salon FAQ

## Salon opening hours
The salon's demo customer hours are Monday through Saturday, 9 a.m. to 6 p.m. Pacific time.
The appointment calendar shows the provider's actual bookable times. Use the calendar to check a specific date.

## Available services
The demo catalog offers Basic Manicure, Gel Manicure, and Nail Art. Use the current service catalog and
appointment availability for live offerings, prices, providers, and duration.

## Booking rules
Sign in as a customer to reserve a future slot. Each slot can have one active booking. Availability can
change before a booking is confirmed. Review the service, price, provider, and time before confirming.

## Cancellation policy
A customer can cancel their own future booked appointment. Cancelling less than five hours before the
start incurs a $10.00 fee. Cancelling exactly five hours or more before the start has a $0.00 fee.
Past, cancelled, and completed appointments cannot be cancelled. The app records the fee.

## Late arrivals
If you expect to arrive late, contact the salon as soon as possible. A provider may need to adjust or
reschedule the service based on availability. Please check with the salon for your appointment.

## Preparing for your appointment
Arrive a few minutes early. Bring any nail-art reference photos and tell your provider about nail
sensitivities and any polish or extensions you already have.

## Frequently asked questions
Times shown in the scheduler use Pacific time. To change an appointment, check for a replacement slot
and manage the existing booking through My Appointments. The cancellation fee policy applies when
you cancel an existing booking.
